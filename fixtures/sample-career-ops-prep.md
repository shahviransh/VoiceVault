# Acme Platform - Interview Prep

## Company Signals
- Ownership of ambiguous problems end-to-end
- Bias to ship with measurable customer impact
- Clear system-design tradeoffs under constraints
- Calm collaboration across product and infra

## Target Keywords
- latency budget
- observability
- incremental rollout
- customer impact
- ownership

## Things to Avoid
- Vague "we" without clarifying your role
- Claiming perfect uptime without incident stories
- Over-indexing on tools instead of outcomes
- Memorized monologues that ignore the question

## Likely Questions
1. Tell me about a time you reduced latency in a production system?
2. How do you decide between consistency and availability in a new service?
3. Describe a cross-team conflict and how you resolved it.
4. Walk me through a project you owned from 0→1 and the metrics that mattered.
5. What would you improve about your last architecture decision?
